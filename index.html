Who We Are

Christ first. People loved. Lives changed.

Our mission begins with Jesus and flows outward through love, service, discipleship and hope.

Living the Gospel beyond words.

Christ Love International is a Christ-centered mission committed to sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ while responding to the practical needs of people and communities.

We believe that genuine Christian love should point people toward Christ while also bringing encouragement, dignity, compassion and practical help.

We are starting small and trusting God to lead every step — from Kenya to the nations.

“In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight.”

Proverbs 3:6

On The Ground

Faith expressed through love.

We want our faith to be visible through both the Gospel we proclaim and the lives we serve.

✝️ Gospel conversations — sharing the hope of Jesus with people wherever God opens the door.
🎓 School and campus mentorship — encouraging students spiritually, personally and academically.
🏠 Children's homes and vulnerable communities — visiting, encouraging and serving those in need.
❤️ Practical support — helping with food, hygiene supplies and other basic needs whenever resources allow.

Small beginnings. Eternal purpose.

We may be starting small, but our desire is big: to see lives transformed by the love and truth of Jesus Christ.
