About Us

Christ Love International exists to make Christ known through Gospel outreach, discipleship, mentorship, encouragement, and practical acts of love. We believe every person needs the hope found in Jesus Christ.

What We Do

We seek to serve people both spiritually and practically, trusting God to open doors and provide what is needed.

✝️ Gospel Outreach

Sharing the message of Jesus Christ and encouraging people to know and follow Him.

📖 Discipleship

Helping believers grow in faith, Scripture, prayer, and their relationship with Christ.

🤝 Mentorship

Encouraging students, young people, families, and individuals to discover purpose and hope in Christ.

❤️ Practical Love

Supporting vulnerable people and communities through food, basic supplies, visits, encouragement, and other practical needs.
